Privacy Policy – Rendell Assistant

Rendell Assistant is a private application used solely by its owner on the owner's own server.

  • It requests read-only access to the owner's own Gmail and Google Calendar.
  • Data is used only to show the owner their own agenda and is stored only on the owner's private server.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
